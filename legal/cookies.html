Cookie Policy

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how YuvinaLoad uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners in order to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

2. How We Use Cookies

YuvinaLoad operates with privacy-first standards. We **do not use tracking, advertising, or targeting cookies**. We only use essential technical cookies or standard browser LocalStorage to remember your preferences (like your last selected download format or quality settings) so you don't have to re-select them next time.

3. Third-Party Analytics Cookies

We do not run third-party analytic services (like Google Analytics) or serve advertisements that plant tracking cookies on your device. Your browsing sessions are kept completely clean and private.

4. How Can I Control Cookies?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though some features or layouts might reset on page refresh.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.
