Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

At YuvinaLoad, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information on our website.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

YuvinaLoad operates as a server-side cache and proxy downloader for Instagram posts. We do not require registration or any personal information to use our service. We **do not collect, store, or log** your search histories, URLs pasted, downloaded videos, or network metadata.

When you click download, our server downloads the requested streams temporarily to a secure, random local folder. Once the file is compiled and streamed back to your browser, it is **immediately deleted from our server**. We do not host, store, or archive any content on our drives.
